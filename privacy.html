Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Morgan's Services ("we", "us") operates an internal snow-removal operations app used by our office and field staff. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app or connect it to QuickBooks Online.

Information we use

  • Account sign-in details for office admins, owners, and drivers.
  • Customer and employee information pulled from QuickBooks Online when an authorized user connects and syncs.
  • Operational data created in the app: routes, work logs, contracts, and job photos.
  • QuickBooks OAuth tokens needed to read company data you authorize.

How we use it

  • To run snow routes, assign drivers, and support office billing.
  • To keep customer and employee lists aligned with QuickBooks when you pull data.
  • We do not sell personal information.

QuickBooks Online

When you connect QuickBooks, Intuit authenticates you and grants this app access only to the scopes you approve. We do not write invoices, payments, or customer changes to QuickBooks unless an authorized user later triggers an explicit action that the product has enabled.

Sharing

Data stays within Morgan's Services systems and Intuit/QuickBooks as needed for the connection. We do not share customer lists with unrelated third parties for marketing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Morgan's Services office administration.
